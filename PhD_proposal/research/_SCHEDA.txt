                                              Context of the research activity

                                                    Autonomous aerospace vehicles are increasingly
                                                    expected to operate in complex, uncertain, and
                                                    dynamically changing environments while maintaining
                                                    high levels of performance, safety, and reliability. This is
                                                    particularly challenging for systems whose dynamics are
                                                    strongly affected by nonlinearities, aerodynamic
                                                    uncertainties, actuator limitations, configuration changes,
                                                    environmental disturbances, and imperfect modelling.
                                                    Classical flight control approaches remain essential, but
                                                    they need to be extended and integrated with advanced
                                                    nonlinear, robust, adaptive, and data-informed methods in
                                                    order to address the performance and reliability
                                                    requirements of next-generation autonomous flight. The

                                      The research will develop an integrated methodological
                                      framework for the design, analysis, and validation of
                                      advanced flight control systems for autonomous
                                      aerospace vehicles. The starting point will be the
                                      nonlinear dynamics of the vehicle, using concepts such as
                                      nonlinear dynamic inversion, feedback linearisation, and
                                      flatness-based design to exploit the equations of motion
                                      and obtain interpretable control architectures. These
                                      methods will provide a common basis on which
                                      robustness, adaptation, actuator management, and
                                      uncertainty compensation can be introduced. A key

                         requirements of safety-critical aerospace applications.
